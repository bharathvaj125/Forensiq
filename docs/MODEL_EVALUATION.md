# Model evaluation against the dataset's ground truth

Generated by `backend/scripts/evaluate_models.py`. Every number below is computed from the labels shipped in `database/seeds/data` (not from data generated for the purpose).

## Case risk model

- Model: `risk-rf-v4-real-labels` trained on 5000 labelled cases ({'Low': 1493, 'Medium': 2406, 'High': 949, 'Severe': 152})
- 5-fold cross-validated accuracy **53.6%** (always guessing the most common class: 48.1%); macro-F1 **0.587**
- Probability of High/Severe (the stored risk score): ROC-AUC **0.895**
- Per class (precision / recall): Low 0.53/0.54; Medium 0.53/0.56; High 0.53/0.42; Severe 0.70/0.94
- Caveat: Medium vs Low/High is noisy in the labels themselves; Severe is almost perfectly identifiable (repeat accused + heinous).

## Anomaly detection

- Method: robust (modified) z-score of log reporting delay, cutoff 3.5
- Flags 51 cases: precision **100.0%**, recall **32.1%** at the standard cutoff
- Ranking quality: ROC-AUC **0.972**, average precision **0.950** (Isolation Forest baseline: AUC 0.868, AP 0.139)
- The 9 officer-level anomalies depend on officer attributes absent from the data and are not detectable by delay.

## Repeat-offender linkage

- Model: `repeat-offender-linkage-v1`, 49115 labelled record pairs from 138 criminal profiles
- Grouped 5-fold CV: precision **99.9%**, recall **98.9%**, ROC-AUC 1.000
- Caveat: negatives include unprofiled records that may truly be the same person, so precision is conservative.

## Gang networks

- greedy modularity over co-accused, shared-vehicle and same-home-district links between repeat offenders (GangID labels not used as links)
- 15 communities; 34 of 34 registry-labelled profiles placed in one
- Pairwise precision **77.2%**, recall **100.0%**, F1 0.871, NMI 0.939
- Sweep of the same-home-district link weight (0 = co-offending and shared vehicles only):

  | weight | communities | precision | recall | F1 | NMI |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0.0 | 12 | 0.125 | 0.016 | 0.029 | 0.716 |
  | 0.1 | 14 | 0.550 | 0.541 | 0.545 | 0.753 |
  | 0.25 | 10 | 0.384 | 0.705 | 0.497 | 0.689 |
  | 0.5 | 12 | 0.593 | 0.787 | 0.676 | 0.808 |
  | 1.0 | 15 | 0.772 | 1.000 | 0.871 | 0.939 |

- Home district is the strongest observable signal (same-gang pairs share a district; AUC 0.98); co-offending and shared vehicles alone recover almost nothing. The weight was tuned on these same labels, so treat the score as optimistic.
